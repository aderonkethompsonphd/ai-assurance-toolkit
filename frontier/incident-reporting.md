# AI Incident Reporting Guide (v0.1 draft)

## What counts as a serious AI incident
Examples: model misuse that causes or enables significant harm, weight theft or exposure, a safeguard failure with real-world impact, an unexpected dangerous capability found after release, a major security breach of AI infrastructure.

## What to require from a provider
- A defined incident process and named owner
- Notice to affected customers within an agreed time (set in the contract)
- Reporting to relevant authorities where law requires it
- A post-incident report with cause and fixes

## Severity levels (adapt as needed)
| Level | Description | Customer notice |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Potential serious harm or major security breach | Immediate, within 24 hours |
| 2 | Safeguard failure with limited impact | Within 72 hours |
| 3 | Near miss or minor issue | In periodic reporting |

## Incident report template
- Date detected and date started
- Models and versions affected
- What happened
- Harm or potential harm
- Immediate actions
- Root cause
- Corrective actions and dates
- Customer and authority notifications made
