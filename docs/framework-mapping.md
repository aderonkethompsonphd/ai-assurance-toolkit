# Framework mapping

Verify clause and article numbers against the current official texts before relying on them in audits.

| Toolkit component | ISO 27001:2022 | ISO 42001:2023 | EU AI Act | NIST AI RMF | NIS2 / DORA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Risk tier model | 5.19, 5.21 | Annex A.10 | Art. 9 | MAP | NIS2 Art. 21(2)(d) |
| AI vendor questionnaire | 5.19 to 5.22 | Annex A.10 | Art. 9, 13, 14 | GOVERN, MAP | NIS2 Art. 21(3) |
| Frontier developer assurance | 5.22 | Annex A.10 | General-purpose AI model obligations (Art. 53, and Art. 55 for systemic-risk models) | MEASURE, MANAGE | n/a |
| Third-party audit guide | 5.22 | Annex A.10 | Art. 55 (evaluation, adversarial testing) | MEASURE | n/a |
| Incident reporting | 5.24 to 5.26 | Annex A.8 | Art. 55 (serious incident reporting) | MANAGE | NIS2 Art. 23, DORA Art. 17 to 19 |
| Financial questionnaire and DORA checklist | 5.19 to 5.22 | n/a | n/a | n/a | DORA Art. 28 to 44 |
| DPA review checklist | n/a | n/a | n/a | n/a | GDPR Art. 28 |

## Notes
- Frontier and general-purpose AI model obligations under the EU AI Act, and the related Code of Practice, are evolving. Check the current position.
- Voluntary frontier safety frameworks published by developers are a useful evidence source but are not equivalent to independent assurance.
