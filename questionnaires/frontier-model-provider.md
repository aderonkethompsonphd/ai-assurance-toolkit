# Frontier Model Provider Questionnaire (v0.1 draft)

**Vendor:** ______  **Models in scope:** ______  **Assessor:** ______  **Date:** ______

For each question, record the answer, evidence reference, and score (0 to 3). Domains match `frontier/frontier-developer-assurance.md`.

## 1. Safety framework and governance
- FM-GOV-01 Do you publish a safety framework with capability thresholds and required responses?
- FM-GOV-02 Who has authority to approve, delay, or halt a model release?
- FM-GOV-03 Is there board or independent oversight of safety decisions?
- FM-GOV-04 How are changes to the framework communicated?

## 2. Capability evaluations
- FM-EVL-01 Which dangerous capabilities do you evaluate before deployment?
- FM-EVL-02 What elicitation methods are used (fine-tuning, scaffolding, tools)?
- FM-EVL-03 Are evaluations repeated for each major model version?
- FM-EVL-04 Can you share evaluation results and methods?

## 3. Independent and third-party access
- FM-EXT-01 Which external parties evaluated the model, and with what access level?
- FM-EXT-02 Do external evaluators have pre-deployment access?
- FM-EXT-03 Can the developer restrict publication of external findings?

## 4. Security
- FM-SEC-01 How are model weights protected from theft and insider misuse?
- FM-SEC-02 What security certifications or independent tests apply?
- FM-SEC-03 Have there been security incidents affecting model assets?

## 5. Deployment safeguards
- FM-DEP-01 What safeguards prevent misuse (policies, classifiers, monitoring, enforcement)?
- FM-DEP-02 How are safeguards tested, including against jailbreaks?
- FM-DEP-03 Do you publish misuse and enforcement information?

## 6. Incident response
- FM-INC-01 How are serious incidents detected and escalated?
- FM-INC-02 What are your customer and authority notification timelines?
- FM-INC-03 Can you share past incident reports?

## 7. Change management
- FM-CHG-01 How are customers notified of new versions, deprecations, and behaviour changes?
- FM-CHG-02 Can we pin a model version?
- FM-CHG-03 What re-evaluation happens before a major update?

## 8. Transparency and accountability
- FM-TRN-01 Do you publish system or model cards?
- FM-TRN-02 Which limitations and known failure modes are disclosed?
- FM-TRN-03 Is there a channel for employees to raise safety concerns without retaliation?
- FM-TRN-04 What is your compliance position on EU AI Act general-purpose AI model obligations?
